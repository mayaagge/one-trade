One Trade — visual direction B — Number line

Level 3, the build step and the reveal, at 375px. Follows your system light/dark setting. Every word and number is the one on the screen today; only the design differs between the three files.

The idea
Quiet while building, then the reveal becomes a number line: the guess is a hollow marker, the answer slides in and lands as a solid one. The distance between them is the whole lesson, drawn to scale.
Web font
One, self-hosted: Inter. Approximated here by the system sans so this file loads nothing; ship it as two woff2 files (400, 700) from the repo.
Spacing and shape
4px scale (4/8/12/16/24). 12px radius, one soft shadow on the card that holds the answer and nowhere else, so the reveal is the only thing that lifts off the page.
What the reveal does
A scale from the level's own minimum to its maximum. The guess is marked first, the answer slides along and stops, and the span between them fills in. The number grows from 0.9 to 1 as it lands.
