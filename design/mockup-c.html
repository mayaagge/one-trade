One Trade — visual direction C — Editorial

Level 3, the build step and the reveal, at 375px. Follows your system light/dark setting. Every word and number is the one on the screen today; only the design differs between the three files.

The idea
Cream paper and a serif for the numbers only. Reads like a well-set textbook rather than a trading app, which is the look that survives being put on a projector in a seminar room.
Web font
One, self-hosted: Source Serif 4, used for the big numbers and nothing else. Approximated here by Iowan Old Style / Charter / Georgia, which are already on a Mac. Body text stays system sans.
Spacing and shape
8px scale with wider gutters (20px side margins), 1.6 line height, 2px rules instead of boxes. Fewer containers: the page is sections separated by rules, not a stack of cards.
What the reveal does
The answer arrives alone on a full-width band, large and set in the serif, rising 8px and fading in over 300ms. The guess is written small beneath it, so the comparison is read rather than animated.
